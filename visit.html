Delivery

Free across Munster, priced beyond it

We deliver on our own crane truck, so the unit goes onto the base without a third party in the middle. Anywhere in Munster is free. Beyond that we price the run before you commit to anything. Lifting over a wall or into a tight garden is extra, and we will tell you straight if the access does not work.

Landowners

If you have ground in a scenic spot, talk to us before you sell it

We are working with a small number of landowners on a different arrangement. We supply the unit at no cost to you, you look after it and let it out, and the income is split between us. After an agreed number of years the unit is yours to keep. It is early days and every site is different, so if that sounds like the ground you have, ring us and we will talk it through.
